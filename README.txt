APW GAS LIMITED — LOCAL WEBSITE

Extract the whole ZIP before opening index.html in a modern browser.
Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js and the assets folder together.
No installation, build step, external fonts or internet connection is needed to browse.
Phone links require a calling app. Email links and the enquiry form require a configured email app. The form prepares an email draft; the visitor must send it. There is no submission server.

EDITING
The BUSINESS block at the top of script.js contains contact details, service copy, FAQs, image paths and service areas. General homepage copy is in index.html. Colours, spacing and responsive layouts are in styles.css. Use a plain text editor and preserve UTF-8 encoding.

HOSTING
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js and assets/ to the website root on a static web host. Configure apwgas.uk and HTTPS through your host. Nothing has been published or domain-configured as part of this local package.

CONTENT NOTES
Gas Safe registration and insurance statements are taken from the supplied business information. No Gas Safe number or accreditation logo was supplied, so none has been added. Stock service photos were selected from the supplied PAH_STOCK folder and are not labelled as APW project examples. The pipework and welding photographs and company logo came from the supplied APW files.
The phrase 'boiler shops' was not sufficiently defined to advertise a retail shop, address, stock or sales offering. The site promotes the described boiler services instead.

PRIVACY
The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or external requests. The privacy notice explains the email-draft workflow. Add any further applicable business privacy information if introducing hosted forms, analytics or other services.
